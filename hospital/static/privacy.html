Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

  • Meddy is a hackathon prototype, not a real hospital service. Please use made-up names and details, never real health or insurance information.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking. The only cookies are the two that keep staff signed in after they type a PIN. Patients get no cookies at all.
  • The AI runs on our own server. What you type is not sent to any AI company.
  • Nothing is sold or shared, and the demonstration is erased regularly.

Who runs Meddy

Meddy is a student project made by Koch Team (Yegor, Denys and Ostap) for the Veritas AI High School Hackathon 2026. It is a demonstration of how an emergency department could work and is not medical advice. Questions or a request to erase something: open an issue on our GitHub page.

What Meddy keeps, and why

From the patient's phone: first and last name, age, sex, how you arrived, what is wrong in your own words, your answers to the questions, and your pain score. For insurance Meddy keeps only a one-way fingerprint of the card number and its last four characters, never the number itself, and a card can have one open visit.

From the staff: vital signs, the urgency level, the nurse's and the doctor's notes, which doctor and room, and the times of each step.

Why: only to run the visit: the line, the right doctor, the phone telling you what happens next, and the chief doctor's summary of the day. The waiting-room board shows ticket numbers and rooms only, never names, complaints or levels. Nothing you type is used to train Meddy's models; they were trained on a published research dataset.

Where it is kept and for how long

In one database file on our server, hosted on Hugging Face Spaces, with a copy in the Space's private storage so a restart loses nothing. It stays there until the demonstration is erased ("Erase everything" on the chief doctor's panel), which we do regularly. "Start a new day" closes open visits but does not delete them.

The AI: Meddy's urgency model and the Gemma 3 language model (which writes the follow-up questions, the nurse's summary and tidies the doctor's note) both run inside this server. Nothing is sent to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic or any other AI service.

Other services the pages use

Hugging Face hosts the site. Like any host, it sees your IP address and keeps technical logs: Hugging Face privacy policy.

Google Fonts provides the typeface (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next), so your browser asks Google for it and Google sees your IP address: Google privacy policy.

Links to GitHub open GitHub's own site.

If Meddy were used in a real hospital

Health information is a special category of personal data (Article 9 of the GDPR). A real hospital would be responsible for the data, would run Meddy on its own systems, and would need a legal basis for health data, a data protection impact assessment, real accounts for staff instead of a shared PIN, and its own retention rules. This demonstration does none of that, which is why we ask you to use made-up details.

Prototype, not medical advice. Meddy never gives a diagnosis.
